Methodology

Identities

FX quotes are INR per 1 foreign (e.g. USDINR). Crosses via INR bridge: units of X per 1 S = inrPer[S] / inrPer[X].

Δ% (session)

Percent change of the INR-per-1 rate (or hard-asset print) vs prior observation in the snapshot. Higher XXXINR ⇒ weaker INR in FX terms.

ECB vs FloatRates

Frankfurter/ECB is preferred when both exist. FloatRates extras are labeled notOnEcb and may lag; never treated as ECB reference.

Hard-asset INR

INR price = usdPrice × USDINR. Multi-day hard-asset history is never invented.

Desk commentary ≠ PPP/REER

Real-strength narrative is research commentary only — not a formal purchasing-power or trade-weighted index.

Technicals / regime / model card

Daily SMA/RSI/MACD, realized vol, breadth, and kx-style KEEP/WEAK/KILL validation from stored history only. Desk summary counts aligned vs counter — not a trade signal. Language: aligned / counter / elevated / depressed / shock.
